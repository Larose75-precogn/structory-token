# Structory Token

> Working notebook for exploring a possible contribution-oriented token around the Structory ecosystem. This repository records ideas and open questions; it is not a launch announcement or an offer to sell tokens.

![Structory Token logo](assets/logo-token-structory.svg)

## Purpose
Preserve the concept, logo direction, hypotheses and decisions in one version-controlled place.

## Initial idea
Explore a token that could recognise and reward **verifiable, useful contributions** to Structory. PreCogn could record contribution rules, evidence, decisions and attribution.

The model should be designed around real utility—not promises of price appreciation.

## Starting principles
- **Contribution first:** rewards should follow useful work and evidence, not merely possession of tokens.
- **Transparent rules:** eligibility, scoring, allocations and changes should be documented and auditable.
- **Progressive distribution:** avoid distributing the entire supply at launch; any schedule must be justified and tested.
- **Capped supply as a hypothesis:** a maximum supply may be explored, but no amount is final.
- **Open exchange is a possibility, not a guarantee:** a token does not automatically acquire a market, liquidity or a counterparty.
- **No promises of returns:** no guaranteed value, liquidity, listing or financial return.
- **Separate idea from implementation:** token design, legal/tax analysis, governance, smart-contract security and launch are distinct workstreams.
