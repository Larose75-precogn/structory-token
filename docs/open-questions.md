# Open questions

## Product and utility
- Which concrete problem does the token solve for Structory contributors?
- Which contributors and users would actually want to participate?
- What real benefits are available, and who funds them?
- Can the model be tested first with non-transferable points or grants?

## Contribution verification
- What contribution categories qualify?
- What evidence is required?
- Who evaluates work, how are conflicts handled, and how can decisions be appealed?
- How are quality, maintenance and long-term value measured?

## Economics
- Is there a maximum supply and what is the issuance schedule?
- What are the allocation buckets and any vesting/lock-up rules?
- How should a pre-seed be financed, and what—if anything—would funders receive?
- How can the project avoid rewarding speculation instead of useful work?

## Governance and operations
- Who can change the rules, and how are decisions audited?
- What wallet, custody, recovery and key-management model is appropriate?
- Which chain or technical standard, if any, should be considered?

## Legal and accounting
- What classification and obligations apply in target jurisdictions?
- Are transferability, rewards, fundraising or exchange access restricted?
- What disclosures, tax treatment, accounting and AML/KYC requirements may apply?
- Which independent professionals need to review the plan?

## Launch criteria
- demonstrated utility with real contributors;
- transparent rules and allocation model;
- written legal and tax review;
- security review and incident plan;
- communication that avoids promises of returns or liquidity.
